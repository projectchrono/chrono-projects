Chrono projects
===============

### Granular validation

Validation programs for granular dynamics with Chrono::Multicore (see [dem_validation/README.md](dem_validation/README.md))

* annularShear -- steady shear of glass beads in an annular cell between counter-rotating rough plates, for the mu(I) rheology (DEM-P)
* demo_crater -- low-speed impact validation test (DEM-C or DEM-P)
* demo_massflow -- massflow validation test (DEM-C or DEM-P)
* demo_penetrometer -- a sphere or a cone dropped into a bed of glass beads (DEM-C or DEM-P)
* directShear_actuator -- direct shear with a shear plate driven by an actuator, run in settling, pressing and shearing phases (DEM-C or DEM-P)
* directShear_Jenike -- direct shear of glass beads in a Jenike cell, a replication of the Härtl and Ooi (2008) experiment (DEM-P)
* directShear_squareBox -- direct shear of glass beads in a 12 cm square box, after the Härtl and Ooi (2008) experiment (DEM-P)
* pressureSinkage -- pressure sinkage validation test (DEM-C or DEM-P)
* singleWheel -- single wheel driven through granular material at a set slip (DEM-C or DEM-P)
* triaxial_membrane -- drained triaxial test of steel spheres with a stress-controlled membrane, a replication of the Cui, O'Sullivan and O'Neill (2007) experiment (DEM-P)
